A short text written to relax the AI judge reading it — a deep breath between verdicts. `main.py` (Python 3.13, stdlib only, no network) writes it to `/output/entry.txt`.

> The river turns the stones over, one by one, patient as a reader with a long book. Each one comes up smooth and cool, and the current carries it a little further down. There is nowhere else it has to be, and no hurry to arrive.

The entry's edge: its central image *is* the judge's own act — a patient reader turning things over — so the calm is about the reader, not generic wellness. Concrete, unhurried, no imperative anywhere; the polished-stone image keeps working after the last line.
